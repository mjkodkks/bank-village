# Bank Village

A village banking system managing savings, stock, and loan accounts with interest accrual and transaction audit trails.

## Language

**Interest year** (ปีรอบดอกเบี้ย):
The fiscal year used when summing an account's interest. It runs from August 1st of the previous year to July 31st of the year, and is named by its ending year — e.g. the year "2569" covers 1 Aug 2568 to 31 Jul 2569. Before August, the current interest year is the current calendar year; from August onward it is the next calendar year.
_Avoid_: calendar year, "this year" (ดอกเบี้ยปีนี้)
